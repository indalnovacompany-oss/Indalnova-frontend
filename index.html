Meet Our Founder

I started Indalnova at just 16 because I wanted to create fragrances that feel personal, memorable, and made with care.

Our mission is simple: to make perfumes and body sprays in India trustworthy, expressive, and affordable for everyone. At Indalnova, we believe every fragrance should inspire confidence.

For us, it's not just about products — it's about creating a lifestyle of self-love and purity. That's why we stay true to our core values: sustainability, quality, safety, and community. We responsibly source our ingredients, keep our formulations clean and toxin-free, and give back through wellness and empowerment initiatives.

Every product is crafted with care, using thoughtfully selected fragrance notes and quality ingredients. Our products are created in small batches for a consistent, enjoyable experience.

Indalnova is more than a fragrance brand — it is our promise to make every day feel special.

— Aniket Singh Thakur, Founder of Indalnova
