Indalnova is an Indian fragrance brand focused on perfumes and body sprays that help you express your mood, personality, and everyday style. We make fragrance easy to explore, wear, and share—whether you are getting ready for work, meeting friends, celebrating a special occasion, or choosing a thoughtful gift.

Our Fragrance Journey

Indalnova was founded by Aniket Singh Thakur with a clear idea: fragrance should feel personal, approachable, and memorable. Our collections are inspired by the way scent becomes part of everyday moments. From an easy-to-wear body spray to a more refined perfume, each fragrance is selected to help you find a scent that feels like you.

Our Mission

Our mission is to offer thoughtfully designed perfumes and body sprays for people who want to feel fresh, confident, and distinctive. We focus on expressive fragrance profiles, dependable product quality, and a simple online experience so that discovering your next everyday scent feels effortless.

What Makes Indalnova Different

  • Fragrance for every routine: Explore perfumes and body sprays for daily wear, occasions, and gifting.
  • Memorable scent profiles: We look for balanced fragrance experiences that are pleasant, wearable, and easy to make your own.
  • Quality-led selection: We pay attention to fragrance character, packaging, and the details that shape your experience.
  • Honest communication: We share clear product information and continue improving through customer feedback.
  • Personal expression: Your fragrance is part of your style, so our range is made for different moods, personalities, and moments.

Perfume and Body Spray for Every Moment

Choose a perfume when you want a more polished signature scent, or reach for a body spray when you want a light, refreshing fragrance for everyday use. Indalnova brings both options together so you can build a simple fragrance wardrobe for mornings, evenings, travel, celebrations, and thoughtful gifts.

Our Promise

We are building Indalnova with care, curiosity, and respect for the people who wear our fragrances. We aim to create perfume and body spray experiences that feel modern, accessible, and worth returning to, while continuing to learn from our customers.

A Note from Our Founder

“Indalnova is my vision for a fragrance brand where every perfume and body spray helps people feel more confident and comfortable in their own style. We are here to make choosing a signature scent feel exciting, personal, and easy.”

— Aniket Singh Thakur, Founder of Indalnova
